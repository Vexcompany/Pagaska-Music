> [!NOTE]
> Pagaska Music is a community-maintained Android YouTube Music client based on the open-source Metrolist project. Pagaska adds its own branding, UI changes, fixes, and project-specific integrations while retaining the original open-source architecture where practical.

> [!WARNING]
> YouTube Music availability depends on your region. If YouTube Music is unavailable in your region, a supported VPN or proxy may be required.

---

## Features

- Stream songs and videos from YouTube Music
- Background playback
- Download and cache music for offline playback
- Audio normalization
- Tempo and pitch control
- Equalizer support
- Synchronized lyrics and multiple lyrics providers
- AI-powered lyrics translation
- Personalized quick picks and search
- Local and synced playlists
- YouTube Music account login
- Android Auto support
- Listen Together
- Music recognition
- Home-screen widgets
- Light, dark, black, and dynamic themes
- Material 3 interface

## Download

Official Pagaska Music releases and APKs are published through the project's GitHub releases.

## Development

Pagaska Music keeps the existing application ID and package structure for compatibility while the project is being incrementally rebranded. Package/application-ID migration is intentionally kept separate from user-facing branding changes to reduce upgrade and data-migration risk.

Build requirements and workflows are maintained in `.github/workflows/` and the Gradle configuration.

## Credits

Pagaska Music is based on **Metrolist** and benefits from its open-source work, contributors, libraries, and upstream fixes.

The project also builds on work from the wider Android and YouTube Music open-source ecosystem, including InnerTune, OuterTune, Better Lyrics, MusicRecognizer, and other libraries used by the application.
